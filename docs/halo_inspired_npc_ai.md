# Halo-inspired NPC AI for Grudge County

This is a design reference for future NPC AI work. It records principles from the presentation transcript *The AI of Halo 1 Combat Evolved | Design & Implementation* and the direction discussed for Grudge County on November 9, 2026. It is not an implementation specification: choose and playtest changes with the current game in mind.

## Design goal

Create the **illusion of intelligence** through opponents whose actions are understandable, interactive, and varied. The player should be able to observe a situation, predict a plausible reaction, try a tactic, and see the enemy respond. Enemies should feel like opponents rather than moving targets. Their behavior can be challenging without depending on perfect aim, rapid fire, or hidden knowledge.

## Principles from the presentation

- **Individual knowledge:** Each actor acts on what it has perceived and remembers, including a last-known position that can become wrong. Vision and hearing create opportunities for stealth, deception, and ambush. Halo used selective exceptions, notably friendly awareness for coordination; it did not claim absolutely no exceptions.
- **Readable cause and effect:** Communicate changes in intent through movement, posture, gestures, and dialogue. Players should be able to connect an event to a reaction: hearing a sound, turning toward it, investigating, then searching, for example. The talk stresses that subtle signals are often missed in playtests.
- **React to the player:** Let enemies be fooled, pressured, displaced, and defeated in ways the player can perceive. Reactions should give the player choices and feedback, not merely make bots more lethal.
- **Variety from circumstances:** Prefer simple, understandable decisions that interact with positions, cover, health, and player actions. Different circumstances can produce different fights without relying on arbitrary randomness. The talk cautions that multiple hidden, continuously changing emotions can make decisions opaque; it retained defensiveness as a useful understandable tendency.
- **Distinct behavior:** Consistent, recognizable tendencies make enemy types easier to learn. Halo supported those tendencies with distinct silhouettes; Grudge County currently has similarly sized cowboys, so behavior and eventual audio cues may help communicate personality in the short term.
- **Spatial tactics:** A position is useful because of the current situation: line of sight, distance, cover, nearby characters, and danger. Halo designers placed candidate firing points; code evaluated them for the actor's current goal. The same point can serve different purposes as a fight changes.
- **Battlefield shape matters:** Connected routes permit flanking and counter-flanking. Open areas with useful cover can give a fight a legible front and make crossing exposed ground meaningful. Cover and routes need to work for players as well as NPCs.
- **Flexible coordination:** Broad group goals can shape an encounter, but individual actors should retain the ability to respond to immediate events. Halo used requests rather than forcing every actor to obey a group action.
- **Playtest perception:** A technically valid action may look broken, unfair, or unintelligent. Test what players actually notice, whether they understand the cause, whether they can respond, and whether combat range and difficulty feel good. Halo reported that toughness influenced perceived intelligence; difficulty is not a substitute for interesting behavior.

## Grudge County direction to explore

These ideas reflect the user's requested direction, not completed work or guaranteed behavior:

- Start with candidate firing positions generated by subdividing each map cell into a **4 × 4 grid**; consider 3 × 3 if 4 × 4 proves unsuitable. Evaluate positions against real movement, collision, line of sight, cover, and other actors. Provide a debug draw for firing points and NPC raycast decisions.
- Address cowboys getting stuck on one another even when a cell has room to pass. Position selection and local movement should make separation and passage practical without sacrificing physical collision.
- Use barrels, crates, walls, and tables as potential cover where their actual geometry supports it. Enemies should seek positions, expose themselves to shoot, retreat to reload, and react to being shot or seeing the player reload. Preserve opportunities for counterplay and avoid making low fire rate or wider spread look like passivity.
- When an opponent is in line of sight, a cowboy should generally face them while fighting or moving, except when running away or falling back.
- Explore procedural indoor layouts with corridors around the edges and larger open central areas. Group barrels and crates, and occasionally use the existing dinette, while keeping routes navigable and giving combatants meaningful positions. Exteriors are a future direction, not the current focus.
- Later, develop barks and communication. In the short term, different vocal delivery and behavior could distinguish personalities such as cowardly enemies even before character art changes. Make a recording list when that work is taken up.

## Working practice

Before changing NPC behavior, inspect the current implementation, tests, and map/prop collision rules; do not treat this document as evidence of current behavior. Make decisions observable in debug tools, preserve the distinction between direct perception and remembered information, and test navigation and tactics in representative encounters. Tune by watching actual gameplay as well as running focused automated checks. Discuss scope and tradeoffs with the user as the design evolves.

## Firing-position debug view

In a match, open F3 and enable **Bot Navigation (Invincible)**. Visible bots show their current action and a thin yellow floor outline at their current destination. The overlay draws only the selected destination, rather than all candidate squares. Human players cannot take projectile damage while this diagnostic is enabled; NPCs remain vulnerable. Turn it off to restore normal player damage. F4 can enable detailed decision tracing in local development.
